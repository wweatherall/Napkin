Autosave is atomic (write a copy, then swap it in) and waits for a ¼-second pause in your actions. Beside the file sits a journal (model.shopsketch.json: actions, results, revision, file hash); the revision is also stored in the file.

Why: Your requirement: always saving. The pause means a quick next action never waits behind a save.
