# I81 — Drawn edges select one at a time, as they were drawn (H211 kept).

Status: Active | Subject: Drawing tools | Principles: G03, G10 | Date: 2026-11-28
Replaces / replaced by: —
Where in the code: viewport/tools/select_tool.gd, viewport/profiles.gd, viewport/tools/push_tool.gd

Drawn edges select one at a time, as they were drawn (H211 kept). Push/Pull works only on a closed profile (a whole circle, polygon or closed loop): when an edit breaks a loop, the profile is no longer pushable and Push/Pull says why (G10). Grouping several edges is done by selecting them and making a component.

Why: William, 2026-11-28 (H211 Q3): 'I do prefer selecting one at a time because that's how they are created. You can always make them into a component by selecting all of them... We just need to keep track of if they break the draft for a push-pull operation... only complete circles work for that, or complete polygons.'
