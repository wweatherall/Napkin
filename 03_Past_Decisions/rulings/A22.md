A board is an App::Part container holding its feature chain: Part::Plane (the rectangle) → Part::Extrusion (the push) → Part::Fuse / Part::Cut (merges and cuts), with a Tip link to the current solid. Components are App::Link, mirrors Part::Mirroring, moves are placements, copies of plain boards are simple Part::Feature copies. All Shop Sketch state lives on the objects (BodyId, SSRole, RectId, SSMaterial).

Why: Native and editable in FreeCAD; nothing kept on the side, so FreeCAD's undo, saves and outside edits stay consistent. Matches the old worker exactly on every test board.
