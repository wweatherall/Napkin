# I48 — Delete / Backspace belong to the measurements box once you click it or type, even after...

Status: Active | Subject: Measurements | Principles: G05 | Date: 2026-11-21
Replaces / replaced by: Refines I46
Where in the code: viewport/main.gd typing, _unhandled_input

Delete / Backspace belong to the measurements box once you click it or type, even after it's emptied, until you pick in the view or select boards again; then, with nothing typed, they delete, in any tool: mid-move what's being moved (a hole being moved is left alone), otherwise the selection. A focused text field (Properties) keeps its keys. Edit > Delete has no menu key (it would fire while typing).

Why: William, 2026-11-21 (T34 review): 'What matters is no text selected then deleted object; when text box selected, it is restricted to that.' 'Once you have selected the text box or started typing then delete should delete the text unless you select the item again.'
