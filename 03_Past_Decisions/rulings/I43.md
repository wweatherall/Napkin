# I43 — Rectangle, Push/Pull and Move start on the press and follow the cursor at once.

Status: Active | Subject: Drawing tools | Principles: G01 | Date: 2026-11-21
Replaces / replaced by: Replaces I29 (Rectangle only).
Where in the code: tools/tool.gd press_started(), press_released(), DRAG_PX, HOLD_MS

Rectangle, Push/Pull and Move start on the press and follow the cursor at once. Started with a press-and-drag (cursor moves more than 6 px with the button down) or a press held over 0.3 s, the action ends when you let go; otherwise it was a click and ends with the next click. Move mirrors on release only if the cursor moved.

Why: Your call, 2026-11-21: Push/Pull and Move ignored the release, so a drag left the action running. One rule in tools/tool.gd for all three. Tested: quick test 'press'.
