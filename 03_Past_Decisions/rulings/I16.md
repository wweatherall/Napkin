# I16 — Axis-locked moves follow the closest point on the locked axis to the cursor; when you'r...

Status: Active | Subject: Move, copies and arrays | Principles: G01 | Date: 2026-11-20
Replaces / replaced by: —
Where in the code: main.gd _move_move()

Axis-locked moves follow the closest point on the locked axis to the cursor; when you're looking almost straight down that axis, they use the point under the cursor instead.

Why: Found in testing: each method alone failed in one case (looking down an axis vs. moving up over open ground).
