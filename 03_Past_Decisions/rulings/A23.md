# A23 — Each action is one FreeCAD undo transaction.

Status: Active | Subject: Files, saving and undo | Principles: G01, G02, G10 | Date: 2026-11-20
Replaces / replaced by: —
Where in the code: replay.py Session.sync()

Each action is one FreeCAD undo transaction. Undo in Shop Sketch is FreeCAD's undo: the worker keeps the common history, undoes the rest, applies the new actions. Failed actions are aborted and leave nothing.

Why: About 35× faster than replaying everything: ~10 ms per action and 9 ms per undo at 120 actions (was 400 ms). Tested with a random undo/retype walk against fresh builds.
