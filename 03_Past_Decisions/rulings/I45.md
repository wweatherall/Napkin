Pushing in snaps to a face behind only when the cursor is nearer to it on screen than to the start or the next face along (and within the snap radius).

Why: Your call, 2026-11-21: on an 18 mm board the back-face snap covered the whole thickness, so a push in jumped to -18 at once. Now it follows to about -8, then snaps. Tested: quick test 'press'; snaptest unchanged.
