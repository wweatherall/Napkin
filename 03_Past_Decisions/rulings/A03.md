# A03 — Faces are referenced by geometry {body, normal, point}, never by FreeCAD face numbers.

Status: Active | Subject: Architecture | Principles: G08 | Date: 2026-11-20
Replaces / replaced by: —
Where in the code: replay.py resolve_face()

Faces are referenced by geometry {body, normal, point}, never by FreeCAD face numbers.

Why: Avoids FreeCAD losing track of faces after edits; boards have distinct flat faces.
