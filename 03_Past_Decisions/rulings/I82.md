Components and arrays of linked copies, the SketchUp way (all copies linked, Make Unique to change one): from outside, one click selects the whole array or component and Move moves it as a group; double-click enters it (I69), and the original (the board the copies were made from, the row's source) is marked faintly; inside, changing any copy's shape changes them all (the tinted siblings show it, G13); moving one copy inside takes it out of the row as a linked copy (the row becomes separate linked copies; keeping the array is T168); Make Unique (U) is how one copy changes alone, and making the original unique promotes a remaining copy to be the source.

Why: William, 2026-11-28: 'Just do what you recommended before and what SketchUp does. Make unique is a fine fallback.' (He weighed auto-unique-on-edit of a copy, and a preference for it, and chose the linked default.)
