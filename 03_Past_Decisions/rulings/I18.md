# I18 — Soft axis inference: drag within 8° of an axis's on-screen direction and the move stick...

Status: Active | Subject: Snapping | Principles: G03 | Date: 2026-11-20
Replaces / replaced by: —
Where in the code: main.gd _infer_point(), _infer_axis()

Soft axis inference: drag within 8° of an axis's on-screen direction and the move sticks to it (dotted red/green/blue line); it lets go past 12°. Snap priority: endpoint > midpoint > on edge > axis inference > on face > free plane. The tooltip names the snap.
