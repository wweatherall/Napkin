# I70 — On a first-ever typed length with no unit suffix (no preferences.json units.default, no...

Status: Active | Subject: Measurements | Principles: G03, G06 | Date: 2026-11-24
Replaces / replaced by: Builds on I67, I62, I59
Where in the code: viewport/measure_box.gd

On a first-ever typed length with no unit suffix (no preferences.json units.default, no settings.cfg units.last, and no saved ShopSketchUnits on the model), do not commit the bare number yet; keep the active gesture live and show in the status bar: "Include a unit on your first length (e.g. 4\" or 100mm); future lengths use that system (change in Preferences)." Once a unit has been chosen, typed, or saved, future bare lengths commit immediately in that system and lock the document units.

Why: William, 2026-11-24: avoid silently guessing units on a brand-new user's first bare length; guide them once in the status bar without breaking the drag.
