# I76 — A fit stays live when its target board is flipped, mirrored or rotated: it follows the ...

Status: Active | Subject: Push/Pull | Principles: G04 | Date: 2026-11-27

Where in the code: Not built

A fit stays live when its target board is flipped, mirrored or rotated: it follows the target's face that is now in that place and faces the fitted board (nearest such face, or furthest if that is what it was fitted to). A default, to be revisited after William tries it (T165, a user test).

Rotation (William, 2026-11-27, for a rectangular target; other shapes later): of the target's two opposite faces the fit could mean, it uses the one that faces the fitted board. Rotating the target, the fit holds the same face up to 90 degrees; near 90 degrees neither face faces the board, so the fit pauses (the board keeps its length); past 90 degrees it resumes on the opposite face, which now faces the board. How wide the pause is will be a tunable when built. Rotating both boards together (an assembly) changes nothing, since the faces still face each other.

Why: Your call, 2026-11-27: 'a good default'; William thought of rotation and mortise cuts and judged it safe, but wants to check it by use one day.
