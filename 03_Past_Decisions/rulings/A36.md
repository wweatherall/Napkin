Where in the code: Built (T64): worker/serve.py watch() / outside() / hold() (a stat per open model every WATCH_S; a change must hold still one look and not be our own bytes; save_pending looks again before each save and skips a held model), the reload and keep_mine commands, keep_both (also a flush, bye or quit of a held model); worker/session.py reload_outside() (keeps the ops of the revision FreeCAD opened, rev_ops or the .prev journal; the rest go to the journal's dropped list; the journal's floor stops undo there in later sessions too), copy_outside_aside() / aside_name(), mark_outside() (outside_sha256, so recover_generation leaves FreeCAD's file alone); viewport/document.gd on_outside_save / send_reload / send_keep_mine; viewport/main.gd OUTSIDE_SAVE_WAIT, _ask_outside (the dialog; a stopped or stuck worker named and logged in worker.log), _on_reloaded (the wave on changed boards). Checks: worker/test_outside_save.py, viewport/test_document.gd, quick test outside_save.

When FreeCAD saves a model while Shop Sketch still has actions pending: Shop Sketch stops autosaving that model at once (never writing over FreeCAD's save) and waits up to OUTSIDE_SAVE_WAIT (10 s) for them to land, then reloads with the wave. If they don't land in time, it asks: 'FreeCAD saved this model while Shop Sketch was busy': Use FreeCAD's version (the unfinished actions stay in the journal as dropped; T65's re-apply offer follows) or Keep Shop Sketch's (FreeCAD's file is first copied aside as <model>.freecad-<time>.FCStd). Both versions always survive on disk. A stuck or crashed worker is logged in worker.log and named in the dialog.

Why: Your call, 2026-11-22: waiting is right, but if the wait fails something is probably wrong, so recover without losing either side.
