When the exact face a fit points to is cut away or deleted, the fit detaches (it doesn't jump to another parallel face); the board keeps the length it had, and the detachment is shown gently, by a small visual metaphor such as a chain crumbling to the ground, not by a harsh 'Reference unavailable' message; it uses none of violet, red or amber.
