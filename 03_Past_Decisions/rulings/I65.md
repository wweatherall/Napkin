# I65 — Numbers typed after creating a rectangle, before any other action, alter that rectangle...

Status: Active | Subject: Drawing tools | Principles: G03, G06 | Date: 2026-11-24
Replaces / replaced by: Matches I14
Where in the code: viewport/tools/rect_tool.gd

Numbers typed after creating a rectangle, before any other action, alter that rectangle's dimensions (keeping its origin, plane and direction signs), just as after-the-fact typing amends a Move (I14).

Why: William, 2026-11-24: after playing with Shop Sketch, typing dimensions right after placing a rectangle should resize it.
