Shapes beyond rectangles (Codex S08): Circle first, then Line and closed outlines, then Arcs, then polygons and rotated rectangles. Circles and arcs stay exact in the FreeCAD model (sketch geometry); only the viewport draws them as facets. Each closed outline can be pushed/pulled into a board or a cut, like a rectangle.
