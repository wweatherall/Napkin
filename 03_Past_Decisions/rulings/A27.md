# A27 — Shop Sketch starts its own FreeCAD worker, checks it every second and restarts it if it...

Status: Active | Subject: Architecture | Principles: G01, G09 | Date: 2026-11-21
Replaces / replaced by: Refines A02
Where in the code: main.gd _start_worker(), _watch_worker(), _on_link_up(), _shutdown_worker(); worker_client.gd link_up/link_down

Shop Sketch starts its own FreeCAD worker, checks it every second and restarts it if it stops (up to 5 times a minute); the lost request is resent. On quit the worker saves and stops.

Why: Your go-ahead (T6): a stopped worker used to leave the viewport waiting forever.

## Details

Decision: Shop Sketch starts its own FreeCAD worker on a free port, checks every second that it's running, and starts it again if it stops (up to 5 times a minute, then it says so). The request lost with it is sent again. On quit the worker saves and stops. --port=N uses a worker started by hand instead (tests).

Why: Your go-ahead (T6). The launcher used to kill whatever was on port 7878, and a stopped worker left the viewport waiting forever. Tested: killed the worker mid-session; a new one was running and both boards confirmed 0.6 s later; after quitting, no worker was left running.
