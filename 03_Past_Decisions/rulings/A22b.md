A board is an App::Part container: Part::Plane (the rectangle) → Part::Extrusion (the push), then booleans, with a Tip link to the current solid. Back-to-back cuts on a board share one Part::Cut whose tool is a Part::Compound of all the cutters; back-to-back merges share one Part::MultiFuse. A cut after merges (or a merge after cuts) starts a new one. Each action is still one FreeCAD undo step. Components are App::Link, mirrors Part::Mirroring, moves are placements, copies of plain boards are simple Part::Feature copies. All Shop Sketch state lives on the objects (BodyId, SSRole, RectId, SSMaterial, SSOp).

Why: Your go-ahead (Q20). FreeCAD's booleans slow down along a chain (Q18). On a 1200 × 600 panel, hole 48 takes 290 ms (was 4.3 s); moving the first of 48 holes 244 ms (a chain recomputed every later cut). Merges use MultiFuse because a Fuse with a compound tool counts overlapping pieces twice. Files made before keep one boolean per op and still work. Tested: test_native section H, all six viewport suites match.
