A rectangle started with a click ends with the next click; started with a press-and-drag (cursor moves more than 6 px with the button down) or a press held over 0.3 s, it ends when you let go. Typing dimensions + Return works in both.

Why: Your call (SketchUp works this way). Tested (--recttest): drag 200 × 100 ends on release; click, move, click gives 100 × 150 and nothing is made in between; a click that wobbles 3 px is still a click.
