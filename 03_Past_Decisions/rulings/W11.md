Highlights and drafts that lie on a face are drawn with a small depth bias in their shader (pulled toward the camera along the line of sight), so they never flicker against the face beneath.

Why: Found by you: white triangles in highlighted faces while orbiting and zooming. Tested: 36 views, gaps gone. A first fix that moved the nodes broke clicking on rectangles, so the bias is in rendering only.
