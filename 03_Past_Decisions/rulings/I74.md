Replaces / replaced by: Answers T159
Where in the code: Not built

push_pull's jobs split into their own ops: a face's end fitted to another board's edge is a `fit` op; creating a board is `new_board` (target, distance, body, optional assembly, and an optional `fit` when the drag ends on another board's edge, so the gesture stays one op and one Undo removes it whole); resizing plain stock is `resize_stock`; pad, cut and pushing a fitted end stay `push_pull`. Nothing you do with the mouse or keys changes.
