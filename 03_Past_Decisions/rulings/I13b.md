# I13b — A typed mirror distance runs from the face you clicked to the plane.

Status: Active | Subject: Move, copies and arrays | Principles: G06 | Date: 2026-11-20
Replaces / replaced by: Replaces I13
Where in the code: main.gd _mirror_gap(), _update_tip()

A typed mirror distance runs from the face you clicked to the plane. A tooltip by the cursor shows that and the total from the face to its mirrored face. If you switch the plane to another axis, it's measured from the board's outer face instead.
