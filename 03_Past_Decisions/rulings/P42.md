A brief for a job that touches saving, reloading or the worker link has a 'What can fail' section, written by the integrator: each step that can fail (a file missing or unreadable, the worker gone, a reply late or out of order, a dialog open while replies arrive), what should happen, and the named check that proves it. The worker may add cases, not drop any; the review checks each case has its check.

Why: William, 2026-11-22 (through fretchup-f9, confirmed here): seven of the first ultra review's ten findings (H092) were failure paths of the outside-save code, which had passed review and the gate; the builder's tests covered only the cases the builder thought of.
