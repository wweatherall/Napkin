Where in the code: main.gd _unhandled_input(), ZOOM_STEP, ZOOM_MAX_STEPS

The mouse wheel zooms by how much you scrolled (Godot's scroll amount), not a fixed step per scroll event: a notch of a plain wheel zooms 12% (ZOOM_STEP), a smooth-scrolling mouse's many small steps add up to the same, and one event never zooms more than 3 notches.

Why: Your report: zoom far too aggressive with an MX Master (it sends many small scroll events per notch). Tested: ten 0.1 steps = one notch. Not yet felt on your mouse: tune ZOOM_STEP if needed.
