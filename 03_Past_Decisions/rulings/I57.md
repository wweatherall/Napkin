# I57 — Lengths are shown in the model's units, picked in a Units menu: Feet and inches (8' 3 1...

Status: Active | Subject: Measurements | Principles: G06 | Date: 2026-11-22
Replaces / replaced by: Builds on I35, T32
Where in the code: Not built: T66

Lengths are shown in the model's units, picked in a Units menu: Feet and inches (8' 3 1/4"), Inches as fractions (99 1/4"), Inches as decimals (99.25"), or Metric (m, cm or mm), each with a rounding choice (1/8–1/64"; 0.1–0.001"; 1–0.01 mm). Default: fractional inches at 1/16". A length the rounding changed shows with ~ in front. The choice is saved in the model's .FCStd; a new model starts with the last units used. Display only: FreeCAD keeps exact millimetres, and typing still takes any unit (I35).

Why: Your call, 2026-11-22 (T66). A framing-style 8-3-1/4 was dropped: it would read as a subtraction when edited (I35).
