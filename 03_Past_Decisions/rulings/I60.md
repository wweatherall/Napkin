# I60 — Arc tool: key A, two kinds; pressing A again while it is active switches between them, ...

Status: Active | Subject: Drawing tools | Principles: G03 | Date: 2026-11-22
Replaces / replaced by: Builds on I49, I51
Where in the code: viewport/tools/arc_tool.gd, worker/arc.py (H085)

Arc tool: key A, two kinds; pressing A again while it is active switches between them, and the hint and tip name the current kind. 2-Point Arc (the default): click the start, click the end, move to set the bulge; typed: a bare number is the bulge, a number with r is the radius (50r). Centre Arc: click the centre, click the start (sets the radius; a typed value is the radius), sweep to the end (a typed value is the angle in degrees). Arcs don't chain: each ends after its last click (William, 2026-11-22). Arcs split faces and profiles like lines (I51) and stay exact arcs in FreeCAD (I49).

Why: Your call, 2026-11-22: both kinds, A again cycles.
