# I08 — FreeCAD rejects a push/pull that does nothing: a cut that doesn't reach the board, a me...

Status: Active | Subject: Push/Pull | Principles: G10 | Date: 2026-11-20
Replaces / replaced by: —
Where in the code: replay.py apply_tool()

FreeCAD rejects a push/pull that does nothing: a cut that doesn't reach the board, a merge entirely inside it, or a merge whose new piece doesn't touch it.

Why: Found in testing: a cut into empty air reported success, and a detached merge would make one 'board' of two pieces. Now these get the red wave.
