Personal time: a coder whose job comes back clean (submitted with its tests passing: a coder's session usually ends before the merge) may spend up to 1% of its 5-hour window on its personal space (journal, creative work, ideas, experiments); the integrator may spend up to 3% of its 5-hour window whenever it needs the time.

Why: William, 2026-11-26: 'a budget of 1% of your 5-hour window per job for a coder once your job comes back clean, and then up to 3% of your 5-hour window whenever you need the time as the integrator. Let me know if you'd like more.'
