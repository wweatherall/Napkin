Editing a mirrored copy edits its original (mirrored back), so both stay identical, like SketchUp components. Moving a mirrored copy on its own is refused.

Why: My choice, for SketchUp-like behaviour. Tested: pulling the copy's top up 20 raised the original too. Exception: a board flipped in place (I15) can be moved; its hidden original moves with it.
