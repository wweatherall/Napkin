# I44 — Drawing a rectangle's second corner ignores a point snap that would leave a side at zer...

Status: Active | Subject: Drawing tools | Principles: G01 | Date: 2026-11-21
Replaces / replaced by: Refines I03b.
Where in the code: tools/rect_tool.gd _rect_drag(); inference.gd on_grid()

Drawing a rectangle's second corner ignores a point snap that would leave a side at zero (its own start corner, the edge it started on); it follows the cursor on the grid instead. Snaps to other geometry stay.

Why: Your call, 2026-11-21: a rectangle started on a corner stayed 0 x 0 for 9 px, then jumped. Now it grows from 4 px (the 10 mm grid). Tested: quick test 'press'.
