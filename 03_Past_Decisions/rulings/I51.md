Lines split faces (as in SketchUp): a line drawn across a face divides it into regions; Push/Pull acts on the region under the cursor, found like a paint bucket from the face's outline and every line on it. The app finds the region at once; FreeCAD gets one op, that region's closed outline extruded (joined or cut), and the other region is untouched. Lines are kept as drawings (saved, undoable) and don't change the solid until a region is pushed. Arcs split faces the same way.

Why: Your call, 2026-11-22: "however you accomplish it in our app to make it fast, you should do that". Region corners come from FreeCAD's real edges and the lines' endpoints, so the join is clean (A03).
