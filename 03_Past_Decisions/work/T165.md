# T165 — User test for William, one day after the fit rulings are built (I76, I77): try fits und...

Status: Open | Covers: I76, I77, T161, T163

User test for William, one day after the fit rulings are built (I76, I77): try fits under flips, mirrors, rotations and cuts and judge whether each does what you expect. Scenarios: (1) a shelf fitted to a side panel's inner face, then the panel flipped in place; (2) the same panel rotated 180 degrees about its length, so its old outer face is now inner; (3) a panel made thicker after the fit (the face moves: does the shelf follow?); (4) a mirrored copy of a fitted assembly: do the copies fit to the copies or to the originals?; (5) a tenon fitted into a mortise's floor, then the mortise made deeper, then cut through; (6) a board fitted to the far face of a target, then the target flipped (nearest vs furthest); (7) the target face cut away by a dado: the chain-crumble detachment, and Undo bringing the fit back; (8) the target board deleted; (9) the target moved so that two faces are equally close; (10) a fitted board copied: does the copy keep the fit?

Result / next step: your call: William tries these when the fits are built; each surprise becomes a ruling.
