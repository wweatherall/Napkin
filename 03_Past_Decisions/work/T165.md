# T165 — User test for William, one day after the fit rulings are built (I76, I77): try fits und...

Status: Open | Covers: I76, I77, T161, T163

User test for William, one day after the fit rulings are built (I76, I77): try fits under flips, mirrors, rotations and cuts and judge whether each does what you expect. Scenarios: (1) a shelf fitted to a side panel's inner face, then the panel flipped in place; (2) the same panel rotated 180 degrees about its length, so its old outer face is now inner; (3) a panel made thicker after the fit (the face moves: does the shelf follow?); (4) a mirrored copy of a fitted assembly: do the copies fit to the copies or to the originals?; (5) a tenon fitted into a mortise's floor, then the mortise made deeper, then cut through; (6) a board fitted to the far face of a target, then the target flipped (nearest vs furthest); (7) the target face cut away by a dado: the chain-crumble detachment, and Undo bringing the fit back; (8) the target board deleted; (9) the target moved so that two faces are equally close; (10) a fitted board copied: does the copy keep the fit? Rotation (I76's pause and switch): (11) rotate the target 45, 89, 90, 91 and 180 degrees, by drag and by a typed angle: does the board hold, pause, then switch to the opposite face where you expect?; (12) end a rotation inside the pause: the fit should stay paused (no chain-crumble) and come back when you rotate on later; does a paused fit read as paused, and save, close and reopen it paused?; (13) rotate the target back and forth across 90 degrees in one drag: does the board jump between lengths smoothly or jarringly?; (14) rotate the assembly holding both boards: nothing should change; (15) rotate the fitted board instead of the target; (16) rotate the target about the axis that runs through the fitted face (the face never stops facing the board: nothing should change); (17) a target that isn't square to the fitted board (a splayed leg) rotated: where does 'facing' end?; (18) a target with a cut that adds a third parallel face (a rabbet) rotated past 90: which face does it pick?; (19) Undo after a switch: does the original face come back?; (20) an array of fitted shelves when the target rotates: do they all switch together?

Result / next step: your call: William tries these when the fits are built; each surprise becomes a ruling.
