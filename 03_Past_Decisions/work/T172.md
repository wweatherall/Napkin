Going forward a job submits its unit tests and adds a check to a dense test, linking its probes

The merge gate keeps running every test meanwhile. Plan (integrator, 2026-11-28; William: plan for all of it, testing velocity first): 1 inventory of every check, file, suite and time (H214); 2 line coverage per worker/tools test file (H215); 3 design 6-8 dense tests, each fast and reporting which part broke (candidates: every op built, saved, reopened and undone against FreeCAD; sync/undo over a long chain; every draft prediction vs FreeCAD, P21; every tool through the quick lanes); 4 tests/dense.json links each dense test to its probes and the runner runs the probes when it fails; 5 a process ruling plus checklist and SUBMIT_JOB change: a job's new unit tests attach to a dense test with links; 6 once T171 is allowed, prove the dense tests against the kill matrix. Steps 3-6 get briefs once H214/H215's numbers are in. William, 2026-11-28: the density works like a fractal or binary search: the ultra-dense tests at the top each link to about 5 less dense tests, each of those to about 5 more, down to tests that pinpoint exactly what failed. Integrator: a failure runs only the failing branches (about 5 per level, 4-5 levels over our ~1000+ checks); a bug can fail two branches, so links form a tree that may share children, and every failing child is followed; a middle level can be a group of its children run together, and earns its own dense test only where that runs much faster than the children; once T171 runs, the kill matrix can build the tree by clustering tests that catch the same mutants.
